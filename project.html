Pedal I: DIY Fuzz Face

Oct 2025 guitarelectronics
A TS‑style overdrive with a switchable germanium diode pair and a bias control for starved grit. Sound clips + schematic inside. Read

This is my favorite kind of problem: minimal parts, maximal character. I started from a classic topology and nudged a few values until it sang on single coils. If you want less compression, flip to the Si/Ge combo and roll the bias back a touch.

Parts list: TL072, 1N4148 ×2, Ge diodes ×2, B100k volume, A500k drive, B50k tone, 3PDT true-bypass, 9V DC.

Bench Supply, Tiny Footprint

Aug 2025 electronicsnotes
Taming a buck module into a quiet 0–12V lab supply with current limit. Panel meter, banana jacks, and a box that doesn’t offend me. Read

Off‑the‑shelf DC‑DC modules are noisy out of the box. A simple LC filter and decent layout get you far. I used a compact Hammond box with a printed label—it’s nicer to live with, which means I actually use it.

Quick notes: add a 2.2µH inductor and 470µF low‑ESR cap on the output, route panel wiring away from the inductor, and float the meter ground if it’s unhappy.
