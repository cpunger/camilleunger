little one

July 2025
About feeling small... Read 

            You are tall.
            Me?
            Yes, you.
            No, I am not.
            No?
            No, I am not.
            I am not tall at all.
            I am very very small.
            How so?
            Can’t you tell?
            Can’t you see?
            It’s in my eyes. Look! Look closely.
            I am smaller than the twinkle
            in a roach’s eye.
            I am smaller than a speck of dust,
            on the back of a fly.
            I could float through air
            and never catch
            a strand of light
            I could drift between brick—
            not colliding with a particle—
            yet, inaudible, shyly say,
            “Sorry! Please! Excuse me!”
            But,
            Though you say
            You are so small.
            I see you.
            And,
            you are dear to me.
            Dearer than you know.
            A million deer would have no chance
            at overwhelming
            your dearness to me,
            little one.
